Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Terms

At Bismi Cabs, we understand that plans can change. We offer a flexible cancellation policy to accommodate your needs.

Local Rides

You can cancel your booking for free up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time for local rides. Cancellations made less than 2 hours prior may incur a small fee to compensate the driver.

Outstation Rides

For outstation trips, free cancellation is available up to 4 hours before the departure time. Late cancellations may result in the forfeiture of the advance amount paid.

How to Cancel

Please contact our support team at +91 9500344749 or message us on WhatsApp to process any cancellation requests swiftly.
