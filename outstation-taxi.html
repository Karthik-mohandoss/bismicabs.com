Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum distance per day?

Yes, usually outstation trips have a minimum billing of 250 KM per day.

Do I need to pay driver allowance?

Driver allowance is typically extra for outstation trips, usually charged per day.

Can we change the itinerary during the trip?

Yes, you can. The final billing will be based on actual kilometers driven and days taken.

Are toll charges included?

Toll charges are usually excluded unless mentioned otherwise.

What are the payment methods?

You can pay via Cash, UPI, or Card directly to the driver.
